How I Got Here

I got into this business in 1999, installing old hardwired alarm panels for an ADT sub-dealer. I was about eighteen — too young to go to the bar with the boys after work. Everything was hardwired back then, and I learned the trade from the ground up, one panel at a time.

From there I worked my way through the industry. I was the security manager for Krazy Kiley's while helping Sasktel Securtek with their home manager service. I did a stint with ADT in Winnipeg, spent a number of years with RMD Security, and ran the service department at Elite Security as their service manager.

Then I stepped out of the trade for a while and became an electrician — a four-year apprenticeship, a Red Seal journeyman license, and years on the tools. I got good at it. I also got sick of getting laid off. So when I heard ADT was hiring again, I went back.

I landed at ADT right around the time Telus bought them. I worked as a field technician — installs and service calls, the work I'd always done. Then Telus offered me a different kind of job: senior learning specialist, creating and delivering training programs for technicians across Canada. I loved it — getting to pass on everything I'd learned, all the knowledge and skills that made me a good technician and installer over the years, to the next generation of techs across the country. I did that for a couple of years, until Telus laid me off.

Their field operations team found out I was a free agent and asked me to come back as a subcontractor. So I did — a lot of work for Telus customers over the years, the same high-quality service I'd always provided.

Then this summer, Telus decided they didn't want local subcontractors anymore. They signed one big national provider and let all of us go.

Why I Started Momentum

Here's what I watched happen from the inside. The big providers run their call centers overseas — you call in and you wait on hold. And at Telus, the service technicians are based in Saskatoon now, so when someone in Regina needs a tech at the door, they're waiting days for a truck to drive down.

I don't think that's good enough.

I started Momentum Security Systems to do it the way it should be done: the same high-quality service I've provided my entire career, from a local tech who actually shows up — me. And without the bloated rates of the big national providers.

What that means, concretely

  • Monitoring stays in Canada. The monitoring station is in Canada, and every agent who answers is on Canadian soil, picking up the phone in Canada.
  • I answer my own phone — 24/7. When you call Momentum, you get me. Not a queue.
  • Emergencies get a local response. I'm based in Regina, so when something's wrong I can be there in minutes — not days.
  • Scheduling takes one phone call. That's the whole process. You call, we pick a time, I show up.
  • No selling you things you don't need. If your system doesn't need it, I won't quote it. I'd rather earn your trust than pad an invoice.
  • Workmanship above all else. 28+ years in, I still take pride in every wire I run and every device I mount. That's the whole reputation.

— Scott Walsh, owner, Momentum Security Systems
